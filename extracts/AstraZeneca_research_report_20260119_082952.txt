### Company Overview: AstraZeneca

**Year Founded**: 1999 (through the merger of Astra AB and Zeneca Group).

**Founders**: The company originated from the merger of Astra AB (a Swedish pharmaceutical company) and the Zeneca Group (a UK-based company).

**Headquarters**: Cambridge, United Kingdom.

### Current Leadership
- **CEO**: Pascal Soriot (appointed in 2012).
- **CFO and Executive Director**: Aradhana Sarin.
- Other notable executive positions include heads of various therapeutic areas such as Oncology, Cardiovascular, and Immunology.

### Industry and Offerings
- **Industry**: Pharmaceuticals and Biotechnology.
- **Therapeutic Areas**:
  - **Oncology**: Key products include Tagrisso (osimertinib) and Imfinzi (durvalumab).
  - **Cardiovascular, Renal & Metabolism**: Notable products include Farxiga.
  - **Rare Diseases**: Treatments like Soliris (eculizumab) and Ultomiris (ravulizumab).
- **Total Revenue**: Approximately $45.8 billion in 2023; aims for $80 billion by 2030.

### Financial Performance
- **Growth Strategy**: Targeting a mid-30s core operating margin by 2026.
- **Investment Focus**: Plans to launch 20 new medicines by 2030, many expected to generate over $5 billion in peak annual revenue.

### Market Position
- **Market Capitalization**: Approximately $292.72 billion (as of January 2026).
- **Competitive Landscape**: Competes with large players like Merck and Janssen Pharmaceuticals, focusing on oncology, cardiovascular, and rare diseases.

### Research and Development (R&D)
- **Investment Initiatives**: Heavy investment in AI to accelerate drug discovery, especially in oncology.
- **Pipeline**: Plans to introduce several new therapies, particularly in antibody-drug conjugates, with ongoing trials expected to yield results in the near future.

### Mergers and Acquisitions
- **Recent Activity**: Acquired Modella AI to enhance R&D capabilities in oncology; actively pursuing other strategic acquisitions to bolster the product pipeline.
- **M&A Strategy**: Focused on integrating innovative technologies through acquisitions to maintain competitive advantage.

### Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG)
- **Sustainability Goals**: Aims for carbon-zero emissions by 2026.
- **Social Responsibility**: Committed to improving global access to healthcare solutions and maintaining ethical business practices.

### Global Expansion Strategies
- **Manufacturing Investments**: Significant investments in new facilities in the U.S. and China to enhance production capabilities.
- **Strategic Focus**: Aims to expand its presence in emerging markets through partnerships and investment.

### Shareholder Returns
- **Dividend Policy**: Has maintained annual dividends for 33 consecutive years as of late 2025.
- **Performance**: Reported a total shareholder return of approximately 35.13% over the past year.

### Conclusion
AstraZeneca is well-positioned for sustained growth in the pharmaceutical sector, driven by a robust pipeline of innovative therapies and a commitment to integrating advanced technologies like AI into its operations. The company’s aggressive growth targets and strategic investments underline its focus on expanding its market share while ensuring environmental sustainability and social responsibility.
