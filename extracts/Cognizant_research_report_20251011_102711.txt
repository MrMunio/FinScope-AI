## Cognizant Company Profile

### Overview
Cognizant Technology Solutions Corporation, founded in 1994, is an American multinational information technology consulting and outsourcing company initially established as a subsidiary of Dun & Bradstreet in Chennai, India. The company is now headquartered in Teaneck, New Jersey, and is part of the NASDAQ-100 index, trading under the ticker CTSH.

### Year Founded and Founders
- **Year Founded**: 1994
- **Founders**: Kumar Mahadeva and Srini Raju (initially as Dun & Bradstreet Satyam Software)

### Headquarters
- **Location**: Teaneck, New Jersey, USA

### Leadership Team
As of January 2023, the leadership team is spearheaded by:
- **CEO**: Ravi Kumar S
- **CFO**: Jatin Dalal
- **President, Digital Business & Technology**: Rajesh Nambiar

### Corporate Governance
Cognizant promotes a robust corporate governance framework requiring all healthcare vendors and subcontractors to uphold strict ethical standards and comply with privacy regulations. The organization follows corporate governance guidelines designed to ensure transparency and accountability.

### Financial Performance
For 2025, Cognizant's revenue forecasts range between $20.5 billion and $21 billion, reflecting strong demand, particularly in AI-based services. The company recorded a revenue of $5.25 billion in the second quarter of 2025, marking an 8.1% year-over-year increase.

### Market Position
Cognizant operates across various industries, including:
- Banking & Financial Services
- Insurance
- Healthcare
- Manufacturing
- Retail

### Acquisitions
Cognizant has a proactive mergers and acquisitions strategy, completing 56 acquisitions as of 2025. Noteworthy recent acquisitions include:
- **Belcan** (approximately $1.3 billion in 2024) - a significant player in engineering services.
- **Thirdera** and **Mobica** (also in 2024), focusing on cloud capabilities and software engineering.

### Innovation and Technology Advancements
Cognizant leverages innovations, especially in AI and cloud computing, to enhance client services. The company has initiated partnerships with major firms, including Microsoft, to advance generative AI capabilities.

### Employee Satisfaction
Cognizant has been recognized for its employee engagement efforts, scoring 3.7 out of 5 on AmbitionBox based on over 56,000 reviews. The company's outreach programs focus on employee involvement in community service, fostering a strong corporate culture.

### ESG Metrics
Cognizant places significant emphasis on Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) practices. The company has committed significant resources towards CSR initiatives, including a recent $250 million investment in various social programs.

### Risk Management
Cognizant employs a comprehensive risk management strategy that focuses on compliance with regulatory standards and ethical conduct. The company has faced scrutiny related to past compliance issues, such as violations of the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act but claims to have improved its governance frameworks significantly.

### Strategic Partnerships
Cognizant has forged numerous global partnerships, including strategic collaborations with firms like Pearson to bolster workforce development and with CrowdStrike for enhancing cybersecurity solutions.

### Regulatory and Compliance Issues
Cognizant has navigated various regulatory compliance challenges historically but has implemented robust measures to uphold ethical standards and protect sensitive data across its operations.

### Conclusion
Cognizant continues to be a leader in the IT services space, marked by significant growth, a strong focus on innovation, and a commitment to employee satisfaction and responsible business practices.
