### Company Profile: Cognizant Technology Solutions Corporation

**Overview**
Cognizant Technology Solutions is a prominent American multinational information technology company that specializes in consulting and outsourcing services. Originally founded in 1994 as a technology unit within Dun & Bradstreet in Chennai, India, the company has evolved into a global leader in providing IT services, with its headquarters currently situated in Teaneck, New Jersey, USA.

**Founders and Year Founded**
- **Year Founded**: 1994
- **Founders**: The company was founded as Dun & Bradstreet Satyam Software, a joint venture between Dun & Bradstreet and Satyam Computers, initiated by Kumar Mahadeva and Srini Raju.

**Leadership**
- **CEO**: Ravi Kumar S. (since January 2023)
- **CFO**: Jatin Dalal
- **Key Executives**: Surya Gummadi, Rajesh Nambiar (Presidents of Digital Business & Technology)

**Industry and Offerings**
Cognizant operates in the IT services industry, providing a wide range of services including:
- Digital transformation
- AI and automation solutions
- IT architecture and strategy consulting
- Business Process Outsourcing (BPO)
- Data analytics and management
- Digital and technology consulting

**Financial Performance**
Cognizant reported a robust revenue of **$5.1 billion** for the first quarter of 2025, marking a **7.5% increase year-over-year**. The operating margin was recorded at **16.7%**, reflecting strong operational performance and effective cost management. For the second quarter, revenue reached **$4.9 billion**, surpassing analyst estimates and showcasing growth in its financial services vertical.

**Employee Metrics**
As of the latest reports, Cognizant employs approximately **347,700** individuals globally, with a significant portion (about **254,000**) based in India. The company's attrition rate has shown a decline to **13.6%**, illustrating effective talent retention strategies.

**Market Position and Competitive Landscape**
Cognizant holds a significant position in the global IT service market and is a member of the **NASDAQ-100**. The company competes with industry giants like Accenture, IBM, and Infosys, focusing heavily on sectors such as healthcare, financial services, and manufacturing.

**Key Growth Drivers and Market Trends**
The main growth drivers for Cognizant include:
- Expansion of AI-driven technologies and automation
- Increasing demand for digital transformation services across various industries
- Strategic acquisitions, such as the recent acquisition of Belcan for **$1.3 billion**, enhancing its capabilities in engineering research and development.

**Client Portfolio and Customer Retention**
Cognizant services a vast client base that includes many Fortune 500 companies. The company emphasizes customer retention through continuous innovation and tailored solutions, evidenced by over **750** early engagements in generative AI technologies.

**Sustainability and ESG Practices**
Cognizant has committed to various sustainability initiatives, investing over **$70 million** in philanthropic efforts since 2018. The company aligns its business strategies with ESG principles, recognizing the critical importance of sustainable practices in ensuring long-term business viability.

**Recent Mergers and Acquisitions**
- In August 2024, Cognizant completed the acquisition of **Belcan**, a significant move to bolster its engineering and manufacturing capabilities.
- Over the years, Cognizant has engaged in numerous acquisitions to strengthen its service offerings and market presence.

**Risk Factors and Mitigation Strategies**
Cognizant faces several risk factors including technological advancements, competitive pressures, and regulatory compliance issues. The company addresses these risks through strategic investments in technology, workforce development, and heightened focus on compliance with ESG-related mandates.

**Historical Stock Performance**
Cognizant's stock has performed steadily since its initial public offering in 1998, becoming a part of both the NASDAQ-100 and S&P 500 indices. The company's shares have seen fluctuations, with a recent closing price around **$78.95** as of June 13, 2025.

**Dividend Policies and Shareholder Returns**
Cognizant has maintained a consistent dividend payout, reflecting its commitment to returning value to shareholders. The company's payout ratio stands at approximately **26.79%**, indicating a balance between reinvesting in growth opportunities and providing shareholder returns.

**Regulatory Compliance and Legal Issues**
Cognizant has dealt with various legal and regulatory challenges, including allegations related to the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) and other compliance issues. The company has initiated measures to enhance its governance and compliance frameworks.

**Global Expansion and Market Entry Strategies**
Cognizant has pursued an aggressive global expansion strategy, establishing delivery centers across various continents. Its approach includes forming joint ventures and partnerships with local players to facilitate entry into new markets, particularly in the Middle East and Asia.
