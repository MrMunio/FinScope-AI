Management Discussion & Analysis

**Fiscal Year 2025 Performance Overview:**

TCS delivered consistent growth in FY 2025 with revenue reaching $29.1 billion, representing a 4.3% year-over-year increase. Our focus on digital transformation services, cloud migration, and AI-powered solutions continued to drive strong client demand across all major markets. The company maintained its position as the world's largest IT services provider by market capitalization.

**Key Financial Highlights:**

Net income grew to $4.9 billion with an industry-leading net margin of 16.9%. Our strong profitability metrics reflect operational excellence, efficient resource utilization, and strategic investments in high-growth areas including artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and cybersecurity services. EBITDA margin remained robust at 23.5%.

**Digital Transformation Leadership:**

TCS continued to lead the market in digital transformation with over 65% of our revenue now coming from digital services. Our investments in Machine First™ Delivery Model, cognitive automation, and advanced analytics platforms have enhanced productivity and enabled us to deliver superior value to clients while maintaining healthy margins.

**Cash Flow and Capital Allocation:**

Strong operating cash flow of $5.6 billion and free cash flow of $4.8 billion demonstrate our ability to generate consistent cash returns. During FY 2025, we returned $3.9 billion to shareholders through dividends, representing a dividend yield of 3.2%. Our debt-to-equity ratio of 0.12 reflects our conservative financial approach and strong balance sheet position.
