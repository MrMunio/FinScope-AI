Management Discussion & Analysis

**Fiscal Year 2024 Performance Overview:**

Deloitte delivered strong financial performance in FY 2024, with revenue growth of 9.4% to $64.9 billion, driven by robust demand across our Audit & Assurance, Consulting, Tax, and Risk Advisory services. Our global workforce expansion and strategic technology investments contributed to improved operational efficiency and client satisfaction.

**Key Financial Highlights:**

Net income increased to $4.6 billion, representing a 11.3% improvement over the prior year. EBITDA margin remained strong at 12.0%, reflecting our continued focus on operational excellence and cost management. Our balance sheet remains robust with total assets of $43.0 billion and shareholders' equity of $17.1 billion.

**Cash Flow and Capital Allocation:**

Operating cash flow of $6.2 billion and free cash flow of $4.3 billion provide strong financial flexibility for continued investments in talent, technology, and strategic acquisitions. During FY 2024, we returned $1.6 billion to shareholders through dividends while maintaining disciplined capital allocation.
